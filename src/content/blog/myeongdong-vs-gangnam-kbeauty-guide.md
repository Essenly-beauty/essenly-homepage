Seoul has dozens of beauty shopping districts, but two dominate every tourist's itinerary: Myeongdong and Gangnam. They're very different experiences. Here's how to choose — or plan both.

## Myeongdong: Tourist-Friendly, High Energy

### The Vibe
Myeongdong is sensory overload in the best way. Streets lined with K-beauty shops, staff handing out free samples, and massive Olive Young stores with English signage. It's designed for tourists and it shows.

### Best For
- **First-time K-beauty shoppers** — The sheer variety is overwhelming but exciting
- **Sample hunting** — Brands literally hand you free samples on the street
- **One-stop shopping** — Everything is within walking distance

### Top Spots

**Olive Young Myeongdong Flagship** — Three floors of K-beauty. The staff speak English, Japanese, and Chinese. They have a "tourist bestseller" section that's actually useful.

**Stylenanda Pink Hotel** — Part Instagram museum, part beauty store. 3CE (the makeup brand) has its flagship here. Worth visiting for the building alone.

**Innisfree Flagship** — DIY mask-making on the upper floors. A fun experience even if you don't buy anything.

### Watch Out For
- Prices can be 10-20% higher than other areas
- Weekends are *packed*
- Some street vendors sell knockoffs — stick to official brand stores

## Gangnam: Local Picks, Salon Culture

### The Vibe
Gangnam is where Koreans actually shop. Less tourist-oriented, more authentic. The beauty scene here is focused on salons and dermatology clinics, with high-end product shopping mixed in.

### Best For
- **Salon experiences** — Gangnam has the highest concentration of premium hair salons in Seoul
- **Skincare clinics** — If you want a professional facial or skin treatment
- **Discovering local brands** — Shops here carry brands that haven't gone viral overseas yet

### Top Spots

**AMORE Seongsu (nearby)** — Amore Pacific's concept store. Beautifully designed, with exclusive products you can't find elsewhere.

**Garosu-gil** — A tree-lined street with indie beauty boutiques, cafes, and salons. The aesthetic is more curated and less chaotic than Myeongdong.

**Gangnam Underground Shopping Center** — Budget K-beauty paradise. Hair accessories, sheet masks, and beauty tools at wholesale prices.

### Watch Out For
- Less English signage
- Salons may require reservations (see our salon booking guide)
- The area is spread out — you'll need the subway between spots

## The Verdict

|---|---|---|
| **Best for** | Products & shopping | Salons & experiences |
| **English-friendly** | Very | Moderate |
| **Price level** | Mid-high | Mid |
| **Crowd level** | High | Moderate |
| **Instagram-worthy** | Yes | Yes (different vibe) |
| **Getting there** | Line 4, Myeongdong stn | Line 2, Gangnam stn |

### Our Recommendation

**Day 1: Myeongdong** — Do your product shopping. Hit Olive Young, collect samples, explore the flagship stores. This is your "stock up" day.

**Day 2: Gangnam/Garosu-gil** — Book a salon appointment, get a facial, browse indie boutiques. This is your "experience" day.

## Insider Tips for Both Areas

1. **Go on weekday mornings** for the best experience
2. **Tax-free shopping** is available for tourists spending over 30,000 won — bring your passport
3. **Don't buy sheet masks individually** — Olive Young sells packs of 10 for much less per mask
4. **Check Olive Young's app** for member-only coupons (works with a tourist account too)
