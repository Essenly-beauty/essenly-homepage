If you've ever watched a Korean hair tutorial and wondered "but what do they *actually* use?" — this list is for you. We asked locals, checked Olive Young bestseller charts, and narrowed it down to 10 products that Korean women keep coming back to.

## Why This List Is Different

Most "K-beauty product" lists online are written by people who've never stepped inside an Olive Young in Myeongdong. They recommend products that are popular *outside* Korea but barely sell here.

This list is based on what's actually flying off the shelves in Seoul right now.

## The List

### 1. Mise en Scene Perfect Serum

The gold bottle you'll see in every Korean bathroom. Lightweight, non-greasy, and works on all hair types. Around 8,000 won at Olive Young.

### 2. Moremo Water Treatment Miracle 10

A 10-second hair treatment that actually works. Pour it on wet hair, wait 10 seconds, rinse. Korean women swear by it for damaged hair from coloring.

### 3. Ryo Jayangyunmo Anti-Hair Loss Shampoo

The green bottle with hanja characters. Korea's #1 anti-hair loss shampoo, based on traditional herbal medicine. Don't let the medicinal smell put you off.

### 4. Daeng Gi Meo Ri Ki Gold Premium Shampoo

Another herbal powerhouse. This one focuses on scalp health and is a favorite among women in their 30s-40s. Available at most pharmacies.

### 5. Mise en Scene Hello Bubble Hair Color

If you want to color your hair in Korea without the salon price, this is what locals use. Bubble format makes it easy to apply yourself.

### 6. Amore Pacific Damage Care Hair Mask

A weekly treatment that costs less than a coffee. Apply after shampooing, leave for 5 minutes, and your hair feels like you just left a salon.

### 7. Essenly Hair Treatment Pack

Our own creation — a deep conditioning treatment designed for travelers. Formulated with Korean botanical ingredients, it repairs damage from travel, hard water, and styling in just one use.

### 8. CP-1 Raspberry Treatment Vinegar

A rinse-off treatment that gives incredible shine. The raspberry scent is a bonus. A cult favorite in Korean online beauty communities.

### 9. Lador Perfect Hair Fill-Up

Protein ampoules that you mix into your conditioner. Ideal for chemically treated or heat-damaged hair. A staple in Korean hair salons.

### 10. Kundalini Honey & Macadamia Hair Serum

A newer brand that's exploded on Korean social media. The honey formula is perfect for dry, frizzy hair — especially in Seoul's dry winters.

## Where to Buy

- **Olive Young** — The easiest option. Stores everywhere in tourist areas. Staff usually speak basic English.
- **Local pharmacies** — Often cheaper. Look for the green cross sign.
- **Daiso** — Travel-sized versions of popular products for 1,000-3,000 won.
